## 📌 Project Overview
Create a premium, modern, professional and fully responsive website for **INSPECTPEAK**, a home inspection and building inspection company serving Bhopal and Indore, Madhya Pradesh.

Brand Colors: Charcoal (`#151E25`), Brand Red (`#C50502`), Silver Grey, White.

1. Website Design

Create a premium, engineering-focused website with a clean, modern architectural aesthetic.

Use a white, charcoal, dark navy and subtle accent-color theme based on my existing logo.

Use realistic, high-quality photographs of Indian homes, residential buildings, structural drawings, engineers inspecting properties, and modern architecture.

Avoid cartoon graphics, excessive gradients, generic AI-looking illustrations, and cluttered layouts.

Add smooth animations, elegant hover effects, subtle scroll transitions and professional typography.

The website must look like a genuine established engineering company, not a generic template.

Fully responsive on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile.

2. Homepage Hero Section

Main heading: Buy Your Dream Home with Confidence.

Subheading: We help you identify hidden defects, construction quality issues and potential structural concerns before they become expensive problems.

Primary CTA: Book a Home Inspection

Secondary CTA: Explore Our Services

Display a premium photograph of a professional engineer inspecting a modern Indian residential property.

Show three trust highlights:

Detailed Property Inspection

Professional Engineering Approach

Clear Inspection Reports

3. About Us

Introduce INSPECTPEAK as a professional property inspection and building assessment brand focused on helping homebuyers, homeowners and property investors make informed decisions.

Explain our commitment to quality, transparency, attention to detail and reliable reporting.
